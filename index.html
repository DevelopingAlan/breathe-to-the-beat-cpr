Who We Are

Hello, Kay here!

I am the founding owner and lead instructor of Breathe to the Beat Community CPR based in Somerville, MA. I decided to take my love of teaching in early education and participation in over 20 American Red Cross CPR/AED/First Aid classes to create a business that focuses on the collaboration between instructor and participants.

One of my goals is to create a community with my classes. A community of inclusion, safety, kindness, and preparedness.

I follow the American Red Cross curriculum, just with my own teaching style sprinkled in. It is a personal passion to work with participants to achieve their goals and work together to overcome obstacles. My favorite moments in class are when the atmosphere goes from stuffy and awkward at the start of what everyone thinks is going to be a boring class, to a jovial and relaxed atmosphere where the class becomes its own entity.

To put this a different way, every class has common elements like course materials and required content that needs to appear each time. What makes every class different is YOU! Each participant brings their own experience, perspective, and knowledge. This is where the magic of community lives in each class. Let’s extend this community beyond class and continue these wonderful conversations. Consider visiting our social media pages and let us continue to build the B2B community.

One of the trademarks of a B2B class is having a CPR playlist play while participants perform CPR. It helps keep rhythm and allows us all to have fun, while learning serious life-saving skills!

If this sounds interesting to you, please reach out in whatever way feels most comfortable for you! You can call or text 857-261-7839, fill out the class interest form on this website, email kayla@b2bcpr.net, or interact with our various social media pages which can be found at the top right of this page. I look forward to speaking with and learning from you all!

Services

Adult and Pediatric First Aid/CPR/AED (In-Person & Hybrid)

This is the most popular class, especially for child care staff and youth workers.

Most state licensing agencies and private organizations require their staff to be certified annually or bi-annually. American Red Cross certificates are in good standing for two years.

Let’s set up a class at your agency to maintain your staff members’ certifications!

Adult First Aid/CPR/AED (In-Person & Hybrid)

This comprehensive course covers a wide range of adult medical emergencies, equipping individuals with the skills to respond effectively. This training also provides a two-year American Red Cross certification and is ideal for anyone wanting to be prepared for adult-focused emergencies. On-site training is also available for agencies looking to certify their adult-focused staff.

Pediatric First Aid/CPR/AED (In-Person & Hybrid)

This in-depth program focuses specifically on the medical needs of infants and children, covering a wide range of emergencies from choking emergencies to more serious situations. This training provides a two-year American Red Cross certification and is essential for parents, caregivers, teachers, and anyone responsible for a child's well-being. On-site training is also available for agencies and organizations working with children.

Refund Policy

Breathe to the Beat Community CPR Refund Policy

Thank you for choosing Breathe to the Beat Community CPR LLC for your CPR, AED, and First Aid training needs. We strive to provide the best possible experience for our customers. Our refund policy is designed to be fair and transparent. Please read the following refund policy carefully:

1. Cancellation by Participants:

a. Full Refund: Participants who wish to cancel their enrollment must notify us in writing or by email at least 1 day (24 hours) before the scheduled training session. In such cases, we will issue a full refund of the course fee.

b. No Refund: No refund will be provided for cancellations made less than 1 day (24 hours) before the scheduled training session or for participants who fail to attend the training without prior notice.

2. Rescheduling:

a. Participants may request to reschedule their CPR training session at least 2 days (48 hours) before the original scheduled date, subject to availability. There will be no additional charge for rescheduling.

b. Rescheduling requests made less than 1 day before the original scheduled date may not be accommodated, and participants may be subject to our standard cancellation policy.

3. Cancellation by Breathe to the Beat Community CPR LLC:

a. In the unlikely event that we need to cancel or reschedule a training session due to unforeseen circumstances or insufficient enrollment, participants will be notified as soon as possible.

b. In such cases, participants will have the option to receive a full refund or to reschedule their training session at no additional cost.

4. Refund Processing:

a. Refunds will be processed within 10 business days of receiving the refund request.

b. Refunds will be issued using the same method of payment used for the original transaction. If the payment was made via credit card, every effort will be made to credit the refund to the same credit card.

5. Non-Refundable Items:

a. Online Courses: Fees for online CPR training courses are non-refundable once the course access has been granted.

b. Course Materials: Course manuals, training materials, and certification cards are non-refundable once provided to the participant.

6. Contact Us:

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our refund policy, please feel free to call us at 857-261-7839 or emailing kayla@b2bcpr.net. Please allow 48 hours for response to email.

By enrolling in our CPR, AED, and First Aid training courses, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to our refund policy. We recommend that you carefully review the policy before registering for our services.
